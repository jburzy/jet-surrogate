What you get

The signal-region efficiency is the predicted fraction of your events that would pass the selection of the chosen analysis. Its definition is set by the analysis:

Pick an analysis to see its signal-region definition.

You also get the statistical uncertainty on that fraction, the number of events and truth jets used, the mean per-jet probability, a histogram of the per-event probabilities and an HDF5 file with the per-event values for your own limit setting. The uncertainty covers the size of your sample only. How well the surrogate reproduces the full analysis is documented on each analysis page.

What the file needs to contain

    Your file is stored only for as long as the job needs it and the results stay available through the permalink. Do not upload data you are not allowed to share with the service operators.
